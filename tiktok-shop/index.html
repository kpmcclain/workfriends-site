Why this matters

TikTok Shop is becoming a critical creator commerce channel. Getting it right matters.

Brand leaders are being asked what their TikTok Shop plan is. Agencies are hearing the same question from clients. But getting on TikTok Shop and building the right model are two different things.

The wrong path, economics, technology or partner can cost time, money and momentum. The right setup can create value beyond Shop GMV through creators, content, paid media and broader commerce.

A $1B heritage brand may need a completely different TikTok Shop solution than a $50M growth brand.

The right answer depends on your business, your team, your economics, your existing capabilities and what you're trying to accomplish.

Four ways to think about it

Build. Buy. Partner. Stack.
